# 🚨 HAPPY BIRTHDAY, ALARM! 🚨
### *The Human Alarm Clock Who Never Shuts Up!*

A personalized, savage, hilarious, and heartwarming 4-page birthday website created especially for **Adarsh (ALARM)**.

---

## 🌟 Features Breakdown

### 🚨 Page 1: Incoming Emergency
* **Theme**: Emergency alert & digital alarm clock aesthetic with deep purple, neon red, and electric blue flashing siren lights.
* **Giant Digital Alarm Clock**: Displays `12:00 AM` with animated pulsing colon, ringing bells, and interactive buttons:
  * **Snooze Button**: Throws hilarious error messages (*"ERROR 404: SNOOZE FEATURE NOT SUPPORTED BY ADARSH!"*).
  * **Dismiss Button**: Gives access denied alerts.
* **Animated Siren & Beacons**: Realistic emergency flashers.
* **Fake Notification**: *"WARNING: ALARM DETECTED. SLEEP PRIVILEGES REVOKED."*
* **Inside Joke Dossier**: *"Known for waking people up during exams. Wanted for crimes against peaceful mornings."*
* **Big Animated CTA**: *"WAKE UP AND ENTER YOUR OWN BIRTHDAY ROAST 💀"* to navigate to Page 2.

### 📊 Page 2: The Official Idiot Report
* **Theme**: Top secret classified government / FBI dossier investigation into ALARM.
* **Suspect ID & Photo Uploader**: Interactive photo frame where you can click to upload or replace Adarsh's photo at any time.
* **Interactive Stamp**: Click to stamp him as **"GUILTY"** or **"IRREDEEMABLE"** with sound effects!
* **Biometric & Psychological Statistics**:
  * Brain cells: 2 (both on vacation)
  * Maturity: 0%
  * Intelligence: Currently buffering...
  * Attitude: 999+
  * Common sense: Premium subscription expired.
  * Drama level: Unlimited access.
  * Friendship score: Surprisingly high.
  * Alarm accuracy: 100% (Unfortunately).
  * Sleep disturbance record: UNMATCHED.
  * Annoyance level: Permanently switched ON.
* **Official Golden Certificate**: *"OFFICIALLY CERTIFIED AS THE HUMAN ALARM CLOCK OF THE YEAR"* with seal, ribbon, and signatures.
* **Unlocked Achievements**:
  * 🚨 Successfully woke someone up during exams.
  * 😴 Destroyed peaceful mornings without feeling guilty.
  * ⚡ Became an alarm clock without batteries.
  * 🚫 Never understood the meaning of SNOOZE.

### 🔥 Page 3: The Roast Gallery
* **Theme**: Chaotic meme gallery of his questionable personality traits.
* **6 Interactive Meme Cards** (hover & click to reveal savage punchlines):
  1. *When he says "I'm coming" but hasn't even started getting ready.*
  2. *When he gives advice but his own life is a disaster.*
  3. *When he thinks he's the main character.*
  4. *When he says something intelligent once every 6 months.*
  5. *When someone asks him to be serious.*
  6. *When he starts explaining something nobody asked about.*
* **The Alarm Chronicles**: Vintage screaming alarm clock illustration with caption:
  > *"Some heroes don't wear capes. Some just call you early in the morning and ruin your sleep."*
* **My Exam Morning Experience**:
  * **BEFORE ALARM (5:58 AM)**: Peaceful REM sleep, tranquil dreams.
  * **AFTER ALARM (6:01 AM)**: Wide awake, confused, phone screaming, questioning life decisions.
  * Caption: *"Thank you for making sure I never got enough sleep before my exams. Truly an unforgettable service. 💀"*
* **Vintage Wanted Poster**:
  * *"WANTED: ALARM"*
  * Photo frame with click-to-upload support.
  * Crimes: *Disturbing peaceful sleep, excessive morning notifications, refusing to let people snooze.*
  * Reward: *One uninterrupted morning.*

### ❤️🎂 Page 4: The Plot Twist
* **Visual Shift**: Warm pink, soft lavender, glowing gold, and champagne luxury lighting.
* **Heartfelt Birthday Message**:
  * Expresses genuine appreciation for his friendship and the exam mornings inside joke, with brotherly warmth.
* **Interactive 3D Birthday Cake**:
  * Multi-tier birthday cake with glowing candles.
  * Click to blow out candles with smoke puffs & celebration fanfare!
* **Floating Balloons & Golden Confetti Shower**:
  * Realistic canvas-confetti bursts on arrival and a "More Confetti!" button.
* **Interactive Surprise Gift Box**:
  * Click to pop open the gift box lid with sparkles.
  * Reveals:
    > **YOUR GIFT: One FREE DAY WITHOUT DISTURBING ANYONE'S SLEEP.**  
    > **VALID FOR 24 HOURS ONLY. 😂**
* **Final Punchline**: *"Once an alarm, always an alarm. Some things never change."*
* **Replay Button**: Replays the entire roast journey from Page 1.

### 🔊 Interactive Audio & Persistent Floating Alarm
* **Persistent Corner Alarm Clock**:
  * Floating widget that rings and speaks funny quotes periodically (*"WAKE UP!", "SNOOZE DENIED!", "YOU CANNOT ESCAPE ME"*).
  * Click it to ring an obnoxious alarm and trigger fresh insults.
* **Web Audio API Synth Engine**:
  * Background birthday synth music (starts paused by default—never autoplays).
  * Airhorn button 📢, Alarm buzzers 🚨, Party cheer chimes 🎺.
* **Photo Customization**:
  * Click on any photo placeholder (ID Card, Wanted Poster, or Celebration page) to upload Adarsh's real photo from your computer. It immediately syncs across all pages.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1. Open a terminal in this directory:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
2. Open the URL shown in your terminal (usually `http://localhost:5173`) in your web browser.

3. To build for production deployment:
   ```bash
   npm run build
   ```
